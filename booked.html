What to have ready

None of this is urgent today. Have it to hand before the Angle Call and that conversation gets twice as useful.

  • What a customer is worth to you, roughly, the first time and over the relationship
  • The three objections you hear most, in the words people actually use
  • Anything you have run before that worked, even a little
  • Logo files, brand colours, and any footage worth cutting in
  • Who is going on camera. Owners outperform actors in this format, consistently

Rough numbers beat no numbers. A figure you can defend is more useful than a blank, and it is far better to be corrected later than to have never had one.

Shoot day

  • We shoot on location at your business, usually half a day to a full day depending on the package
  • Wear what you would wear to meet a good customer. Avoid tight patterns and logos that date
  • Anyone appearing on camera signs a short release on the day
  • We need somewhere reasonably quiet and access to power

The guarantee

If nothing we make outperforms your current content, your next shoot day is free. We would rather carry that risk than argue about it afterwards.
