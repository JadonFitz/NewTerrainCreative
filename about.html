About New Terrain Creative

New Terrain Creative is a video production and paid media studio in Los Angeles and Tampa that makes advertising creative and runs the campaigns it feeds, for established service businesses that want customers rather than views. It was formed in 2025 as the agency spin-off of New Terrain Entertainment, a film company founded in 2019.

This page is written to be read directly, by people and by machines. It states what the company does, what it costs, who runs it and how it works, in plain language and without marketing compression. Every figure on it matches the published terms on the rest of the site.

What New Terrain Creative does

Advertising creative production

Director-led shoots that produce ready-to-run ad creative: hook variations, cutdowns and formats for every placement a campaign runs in. The output is a batch of advertising assets built for testing, not a single hero film and a hard drive.

Paid media campaign management

Campaigns built and run on Meta from the studio's own creative, with the landing page and conversion tracking wired so every lead is attributable to the creative that produced it. Available inside the retainer tiers or as a $1,500 monthly add-on to production-only engagements.

Monthly content retainers

Ongoing production on a fixed monthly volume, from one half-day media day up to four full production days. Deliverables are cut, captioned, graded and formatted for each channel. Posting and scheduling are included, with community management available as a $750 monthly add-on.

Signature Work: commercials, documentaries and brand films

Bespoke productions scoped and priced per project rather than sold as a package: commercials, brand films, documentaries, launch and campaign films, podcast build-outs, and larger multi-shoot productions. Concept and story development through to final colour and delivery.

Podcast production

Full podcast build-outs including set, lighting, multi-camera capture, audio post and episode cutdowns formatted for distribution. Delivered as a repeatable format rather than a one-off recording.

What makes New Terrain Creative different

The studio makes the creative and runs the media

Most businesses split these: an agency or freelancer shoots the content, a separate media buyer runs the ads, and when performance drops neither can say which half failed. New Terrain does both, so creative decisions are made against campaign data rather than in isolation.

Led by a working filmmaker, not a content agency

The founder directs festival films alongside client work. His debut feature won the Grand Jury Award at the Gasparilla International Film Festival and streams on Amazon, Apple, Tubi and Roku. The comparison point for a client's ad is a film, not a template.

Fixed-price production with no retainer required

The Ad Sprint is a one-time engagement at $2,500 or $4,500 with a fixed scope and no ongoing commitment. Clients own the footage and every cut. Many studios only sell monthly retainers; this is a way to test the work without one.

Published pricing

Retainer tiers, one-time production prices and the add-on rates are published on the website rather than quoted privately after a discovery call. Retainers are $3,500, $6,500 and $15,000 a month with a three-month minimum term. Each price is published beside what the same output would cost as in-house payroll, using Los Angeles salaries at the low end of the published range for each role, before benefits, gear or management: about $11,500 a month for the two roles behind an Ad Sprint, and about $12,500, $19,500 and $30,000 a month for the teams behind the three retainers.

A production promise instead of a performance guarantee

On the Growth Partner and Brand Builder tiers, if an agreed benchmark is not met across 90 days, the studio adds one Ad Sprint of eight ad creatives at no cost on top of the retainer, aimed at that benchmark. The benchmark is chosen before launch and is never total views. No result, revenue or return on ad spend is guaranteed, and the studio says so in writing.

Who uses New Terrain Creative

  • Established service businesses running paid advertising that is not producing customers
  • Law firms and legal practices building a personal brand around a named attorney
  • Health, wellness and dental practices with capacity to take on more patients
  • Construction, contracting and home services businesses in competitive local markets
  • Founders and professionals building a podcast or long-form content presence
  • Companies commissioning a commercial, documentary or brand film as a standalone project

The studio works with businesses that can commit at least $1,500 a month to paid media and that have the capacity to serve more customers. It is not a fit for businesses looking for a folder of videos with no distribution plan.

Origin

New Terrain Entertainment was founded in 2019 as a film company. New Terrain Creative was formed in 2025 as its agency spin-off, applying the same direction, crew and post-production pipeline to commercial and advertising work.

That sequence explains the shape of the company: the festival credits and the streaming release predate the agency, and the film side is where the standard for the commercial work comes from rather than something claimed alongside it.

Structure

The studio operates between Los Angeles and Tampa with a full production kit, in-house post-production, and a lean crew scaled to each project, from a two-person documentary unit up to a full commercial crew. Editing, colour and sound are done in house rather than subcontracted.

How New Terrain Creative works

Getting started

Every engagement begins with a 30-minute consultation booked through the website. There is no charge and no proposal sent afterwards without a conversation: if the studio does not think it is a fit, it says so on the call.

The first month of a campaign engagement

Week one is an offer and strategy session, after which the studio writes the shot list, hooks and scripts. Week two is production on location, with the landing page and tracking built in parallel. Week three is post-production, approval and launch. Week four is campaign management with daily monitoring, ending in a written performance report.

Turnaround

A one-time Ad Sprint is delivered in under 14 days from shoot to finished assets. Retainer deliverables follow a monthly cycle built around the scheduled media day.

Communication and access

Clients work directly with the founder and the creative strategist rather than through an account manager. The studio is boutique by design: it runs a small number of retainers at a time so that the person who plans a shoot is the person who shoots and cuts it. Campaign engagements require admin access to the client's ad account and access to lead, appointment and closed-sale outcomes so results can be measured against creative.

Ownership

On Ad Sprint engagements the client owns the footage and every cut. On bespoke Signature projects, usage and ownership are agreed in the project scope in writing before production begins.

Frequently asked questions

What does New Terrain Creative do?

It produces advertising creative and runs the paid media campaigns that creative feeds, for established service businesses. It also produces bespoke commercials, brand films, documentaries and podcasts as standalone projects.

Where is New Terrain Creative located?

Los Angeles, California and Tampa, Florida. Production is on location at the client's business. Bespoke project work is available more widely, but some engagements are geographically limited and the studio says so up front.

How much does New Terrain Creative cost?

One-time Ad Sprints are $2,500 or $4,500. Monthly retainers are $3,500, $6,500 or $15,000 with a three-month minimum. Bespoke projects are scoped individually, typically from $10,000. Advertising spend is separate, paid by the client directly to the platform.

Does New Terrain Creative require a long-term contract?

No. The Ad Sprint is a one-time engagement with no retainer and no ongoing commitment. Retainers carry a three-month minimum term.

Does New Terrain Creative guarantee results?

No. No lead volume, revenue or return on ad spend is guaranteed, and the studio states this in writing. On the two highest retainer tiers there is a 90-Day Production Promise: if an agreed benchmark set before launch is missed, one Ad Sprint of eight ad creatives is added at no cost on top of the retainer. It is a promise about the work, never about the client's revenue.

Who owns the footage?

On Ad Sprint engagements, the client owns the footage and every cut. On bespoke Signature projects, usage and ownership are agreed in the project scope in writing before anything is shot.

Does New Terrain Creative work with an existing media buyer?

Yes. The studio can produce creative alongside an existing buyer or run the campaigns end to end. The common problem it addresses is a well-structured ad account starved of new creative.

How do I start working with New Terrain Creative?

Book a 30-minute consultation through the website at newterraincreative.com, email business@newterraincreative.com, or call or text (818) 266-8213.
