How It Runs

One day on location.
Running in about a week.

I
Day 1
The Angle Call

Forty-five minutes on what you sell, who says no and why, and what has already worked. You leave with a shot list and a set of hooks worth testing. Nothing gets shot before this is settled, and that is the whole reason a week is possible.

II
Day 2 or 3
The Shoot

One day at your place. Me, a camera, and a light if the room needs it. No crew standing around, which is exactly why people stop performing and start sounding like themselves. We shoot every hook in one block.

III
Day 4 to 6
The Cut

Every creative built out, captioned, and exported for each placement. Vertical, square, and horizontal, with the hooks split out so you can test them against each other independently. Licensed music where a track helps and silence where it does not.

IV
Day 7
Running

Files in your hands with a one-page testing plan: what to run first, what to spend, what to watch, and when to call a winner. Your first ads can be live the same day. Run them yourself or have us run them.

Straight Answers

What you are
probably thinking.

I already have someone making my content.

Then you are most of the way there, and this is a different job. Content keeps an audience warm. Ads have to stop a stranger and sell them something, which is a separate craft with separate rules. Most content people were never asked to do it, and it is not a knock on them that it shows.

A week sounds rushed.

It is fast because the planning happens before anyone picks up a camera, and because there is no crew to schedule around. By the shoot day the hooks are written and the shot list is locked, so the day is execution instead of discovery. The speed comes out of the process, not out of the work.

What if none of them work?

Then your next shoot day is free. We are betting another full day of our time that at least one of these beats what you are running now, and that is a bet we would rather make than argue about.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Plenty of the strongest performers are product-led, customer-led, or built from footage with no founder in frame at all. If you do want to be the face, one person with a camera is about as painless as it gets, and it is a large part of why these come out sounding natural. The Angle Call is where we decide which version you are actually doing.

I am not in Los Angeles or Tampa.

We work with clients worldwide. Travel outside Los Angeles or Tampa gets quoted on top, and we will tell you what it costs on the call before you commit to anything. For some businesses the better answer is a hybrid: we direct remotely and cut from footage shot on your end.

Will you run the ads too?

Not inside the Sprint. This gets you the creative and the plan to test it. If you would rather hand off the whole thing, we run Meta and Apple Maps as a monthly engagement, and most people make that call after they have seen what the first set does.
