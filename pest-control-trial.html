How we keep it fast

What's AI.
What isn't.

An AI-assisted workflow is how five days and a flat rate are possible. It never pretends to be your business.

AI helps with

  • Cutting and captions
  • Motion graphics and statics
  • Voiceover
  • Generic illustrative b-roll to fill gaps

AI never makes

  • Your name or logo on generated imagery that passes as your real operation
  • Fake techs presented as your team
  • Fake before and afters
  • Invented reviews

Illustrative is fine. Impersonating your business is not: it misrepresents you, and it gets ads rejected.
