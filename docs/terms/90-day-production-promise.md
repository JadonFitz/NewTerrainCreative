# The 90-Day Production Promise

> ## NOT ATTORNEY-APPROVED
>
> This is a drafting document prepared for attorney review. It has not
> been reviewed by counsel and must not be published, linked, attached to
> an agreement, or shown to a client until it has been. The public-facing
> sentences in section 1 are already rendered on the website; everything
> from section 2 down is not, and must not be, until review is complete.
>
> Drafted 12 September 2026. Supersedes the earlier total-views guarantee,
> which is withdrawn and must not be reinstated in any form.

## 1 · Public language

Headline, verbatim, on `/grow`:

> **THE 90-DAY PRODUCTION PROMISE**
>
> If, after 90 consecutive days, the campaign has not improved the primary
> performance benchmark we agree upon during onboarding, the next standard
> production cycle is on us.

Supporting sentence, permitted anywhere the short form is needed:

> If the agreed campaign benchmark does not improve during the first 90
> days, month four's standard production is on us.

Nothing else may be said publicly about this promise without the full
terms available alongside it.

## 2 · What is being promised, in one line

One further production cycle at no charge, if an agreed and measurable
campaign benchmark has not improved over a complete, properly run 90-day
period.

It is a promise about **our work**, not about the client's business
outcome. That distinction is the whole design and every clause below
protects it.

## 3 · The benchmark

3.1 A single primary KPI is selected and documented in the signed scope
**before launch**. One. Not a basket, not "whichever moves least".

3.2 The baseline value and the measurement source are documented **before
the campaign begins**. A benchmark with no recorded starting point cannot
improve or fail to improve, and a promise resting on one is unenforceable
in both directions.

3.3 Eligible benchmarks include, and are limited to, what the parties
agree in writing from:

- qualified cost per lead
- qualified-lead rate
- landing-page conversion rate
- click-through rate
- another mutually agreed measurable indicator, documented in the scope

3.4 **Total views are not automatically the benchmark** and may only be
selected where both parties agree in writing that it is the right measure
for that engagement. The prior guarantee was built on views and that is
precisely why it was withdrawn.

3.5 "Improved" means a change in the favourable direction against the
documented baseline, measured at the documented source, over the complete
measurement period. The parties should record what magnitude, if any, is
required. *[Open: see section 8.]*

## 3A · Which engagements the promise attaches to

**DECIDED 12 September 2026.** This was open question 6 and is now settled.

3A.1 The promise applies to **Growth Partner** and **Brand Builder** only.

3A.2 It does **not** apply to **The Anchor**, with or without the
campaign-management add-on.

3A.3 The reason is clause 4.6. The promise requires that New Terrain
managed the campaign throughout the measurement period. Anchor is
production only, so every claim would fail 4.6 automatically. Anchor plus
the add-on gives us management of a fixed batch of creative with no
iteration shoot and no variant budget, so the lever the remedy assumes,
changing the creative in response to the data, is not in scope either.

3A.4 A promise offered where it can never pay out is worse than offering
none: it is a claim we would be relying on a technicality to defeat.
Counsel should treat 3A.2 as a term to state plainly in the Anchor
agreement, not merely omit.

3A.5 The eligibility is encoded in `assets/offer.js` under
`productionPromise.eligiblePackages`, and in `api/_rates.js` per tier as
`productionPromiseEligible`.

## 4 · Client conditions

The promise is available only where all of the following held for the
whole measurement period. Each exists because it is a way the measurement
can be destroyed, not as an escape hatch.

4.1 The client maintained the agreed minimum advertising spend.

4.2 The required tracking remained operational.

4.3 The campaign remained active for the complete measurement period.

4.4 The client provided approvals and requested performance data in a
timely manner.

4.5 The client did not materially pause or change the offer, funnel,
campaign or targeting in a way that invalidates measurement.

4.6 New Terrain Creative managed the campaign throughout the measurement
period.

## 5 · The remedy

5.1 One defined standard production cycle, matching the contracted monthly
production allocation, **subject to a maximum value documented in the
agreement**.

5.2 Includes the defined production component and **one** standard
revision round.

5.3 Excludes, without exception: advertising spend, campaign-management
fees, software, talent, locations, travel, permits, props, media
purchases, and any third-party expense.

5.4 A one-time service credit. **Not a cash refund**, not transferable,
not exchangeable for a fee reduction.

5.5 **Cannot be stacked with the Founding Three waived first month.** A
Founding Three client has already received a production cycle at no
service charge; the promise does not entitle them to a second. This must
be stated in the Founding Three agreement as well as here, because it is
the term a founding client is most likely to be surprised by.

## 6 · What is not promised

Leads, revenue, sales, ROAS, virality, or any other business result. The
promise is that if our work did not move an agreed measure, we do more
work. It is not a warranty of commercial outcome and must never be
described as one, in copy, on a call, or in a proposal.

## 7 · Availability

Full terms must be available to the client **before** the agreement is
signed. A headline on a web page is not the term; it is a pointer to it.

## 8 · Open questions for counsel and for Jadon

1. **Magnitude.** Clause 3.5 says "improved" without saying by how much.
   Any improvement, however small, currently defeats a claim, which is
   arguably in bad faith. A stated threshold is fairer and much harder to
   argue about later. This is a commercial decision before it is a legal
   one.
2. **Maximum value.** Clause 5.1 requires a documented cap. No figure is
   set. At Brand Builder rates an uncapped production cycle is a
   materially different exposure than at Anchor.
3. **Who judges.** Nothing says who determines whether conditions 4.1 to
   4.6 were met, or what happens when the parties disagree.
4. **Qualified.** Two eligible benchmarks turn on "qualified" leads. That
   word needs a definition in the scope or it becomes the argument.
5. **Claim window.** No deadline for a client to invoke the promise.
   Suggest a fixed number of days after the 90-day period ends.
6. ~~**Which packages.**~~ **Resolved, see section 3A.** Growth Partner
   and Brand Builder only. Counsel should confirm that stating the
   exclusion in the Anchor agreement is preferable to silence.
