## Rules this script is written under

- The words "VSL" and "funnel" never appear.
- No rate card. `/grow` publishes one capacity signal and nothing more
  precise, and this video says exactly the same thing. Exact scope and
  fee are quoted after a conversation, and they live in `api/_rates.js`,
  server side.
- The founding offer is never mentioned. Someone who hires at full rate
  should not learn from this video that three other people got a month
  without a service fee.
- No guaranteed leads, revenue or return.
- This is a different buyer, not a cheaper one. They are not being
  qualified out of the free month; they were never in that queue.
- No Apple Maps.
- No b-roll direction.

## THE PROBLEM · 0:20 → 1:02

Most businesses don't have one marketing problem. They have a
disconnected one.

The content gets made. The ads get launched. Somebody fills out your form
at 8:47 on a Tuesday night, and nobody sees it until Wednesday morning.
By Wednesday morning they've already called somebody else.

And because the pieces are separate, nobody can tell you which part
worked. So you can't fix it. You can only spend more.

So we start somewhere else. Not with what content should we make. With
what are we trying to get a customer to do. Then we build backwards.

## WHAT WE ACTUALLY DO · 1:02 → 1:44

Offer. Creative. Distribution. Conversion.

First we get the offer clear: who it's for, what it solves, what you want
them to do next.

Then we build the creative around that offer. Not content in general.
Creative with a job.

Then we put money behind it, so it reaches the right market instead of
waiting on an algorithm to feel generous.

Then we make sure the lead has somewhere to land and reaches your team
while the person is still interested. Not the next morning.

## WHY IT MATTERS · 1:44 → 2:04

A good video on its own isn't a marketing strategy. Neither is running

An ad can't fix a weak offer. Good media buying can't fix bad creative.
And nothing fixes a lead nobody called back.

That's why they're one thing here instead of three vendors.

## HOW WE WORK · 2:04 → 2:48

We work as one partner across the whole thing, on a monthly retainer,
with a three month minimum.

Three months because the first month is production and launch, the second
is the first honest read on what's working, and the third is where you
act on it. Two months of that is a shoot and a shrug. We'd rather turn
down the work than sell you a month that can't tell you anything.

Most of the businesses we work like this with are health and wellness,
law, dental, and construction and contracting. Not because we can't shoot
anything else. Because those four have a customer worth enough that paid
media makes arithmetic sense, and we've stopped pretending that's true of
every business.

## WHAT IT COSTS · 2:48 → 3:22

Partnerships start in the mid four figures a month, and I'm not going to
put a rate card in a video, because I'd be quoting you before I know what
you need.

What I'll do on a call is tell you what we'd actually do, roughly what
that costs, and whether I think it's worth doing. If the honest answer is
that you'd be better off spending that money somewhere else this quarter,
you'll get that answer on the call instead of a proposal a week later.

Your ad spend is separate and stays yours, on your account, on your card.
