03 How Custom Projects Run

No two of these
are the same.

Which is why the process starts with a brief instead of a package. Five stages, and you know what happens in each one before anything is booked.

I
The Brief

You tell us what the project is, who it has to move, and when it has to exist. That happens in writing first, on purpose: a written brief is worth more than a discovery call, and it means the first conversation starts somewhere useful instead of at the beginning.

II
Concept & Treatment

Before a camera turns on, we figure out what makes people choose you, then build every shot around it. You see the idea, the visual references and the shape of the piece in a treatment. No generic b-roll packages, no "we'll find it in the edit."

III
Production

Director-led shoots where the person behind the camera is the same one who built the plan. No handoffs, no gaps in vision. Crew sized to the project, from a two-person documentary unit up to a full commercial crew.

IV
Post

Edit, color and sound in house. We own the look from first frame to last, which is why the grade on a brand film matches the grade on a feature.

V
Delivery

Masters plus every aspect ratio, cutdown and caption file the release actually runs in. Usage and ownership are agreed in the scope, in writing, before anything is shot. If the piece has a second life as ads or social, it was framed for that from the treatment onward.

The through line is that one person carries the project from the brief to the master. That is the whole reason this work is called Signature.

05 Project Fit

Worth saying out loud
before you write in.

Signature Work is not the right answer to every video problem, and the fastest way to waste both our time is to find that out on a call.

This is the right room when
  • ✓The piece carries real weight: a launch, a raise, a rebrand, a film that has to represent you for years
  • ✓You want a point of view on the story, not a crew who shoots what they are told
  • ✓Craft is the point. If it looked like everyone else's, it would not be worth making
  • ✓You have a decision maker in the room and a date the work has to exist by
  • ✓The project deserves a treatment before a quote
Something else fits better when
  • —What you actually need is a batch of ads to test. That is the Ad Sprint, and it is a fixed price
  • —You need consistent monthly volume across your channels. That is a growth retainer
  • —The deliverable is already fully specified and you are collecting bids on it
  • —The timeline is days. Custom work is scoped, treated and scheduled, and that takes longer than a shoot
